Born from Defiance

The Raging Bull Heritage

In 1963, Ferruccio Lamborghini resolved to craft the world's most ferocious and technically advanced sports cars. Explore sixty years of legendary milestones.

1966

Miura P400

Designed by Marcello Gandini, the Miura introduced the transverse mid-engine V12 layout, coining the very term "supercar" and leaving competitors stunned.

1974

Countach LP400

The wedge-shaped futuristic sensation with pioneering scissor doors. A design so daring it adorned bedroom posters worldwide for over two decades.

1990

Diablo

The first production Lamborghini capable of exceeding 200 mph (325 km/h). Later equipped with the VT (Viscous Traction) all-wheel drive system.

2003

Gallardo

The legendary high-revving 5.0L and 5.2L V10 that redefined usability, AWD traction, and commercial success with over 14,000 units delivered globally.

2011

Aventador

Full carbon fiber monocoque passenger cell, pushrod suspension, and pure naturally aspirated 6.5L V12 generating up to 770 CV in the ultimate SVJ edition.

Today

Revuelto

The dawn of Direzione Cor Tauri: the V12 High Performance Electrified Vehicle (HPEV) delivering 1,015 CV with zero carbon compromise.
